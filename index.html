How it works

Three steps between a number and a table.

01

Set the number

Type what you want to spend tonight, or across the whole weekend. One figure in dirhams, nothing vaguer than that.

02

Get two or three plans

Each plan is complete and itemised: the meal, the activity, the room, the drive, the parking. The total sits at the bottom.

03

Book it in the app

Reserve the table and the tickets from inside the plan. The number you agreed to is the number you pay.
